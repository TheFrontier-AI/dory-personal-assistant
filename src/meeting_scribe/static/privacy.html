Dory Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Operated by The Frontier Company Limited ("we").

Dory (ดอรี่) is an AI coworker that works inside the chat tools your organization uses (Discord, Slack, LINE, Telegram, WhatsApp) and in the Dory web command center. This policy explains what data Dory processes, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we process

  • Account data: your name and email address from Google or Discord sign-in, and the chat accounts you link to your Dory profile.
  • Workspace content: messages, files, receipts and meeting notes in rooms where your organization has enabled Dory, so it can answer questions and keep team memory. Your organization chooses which rooms Dory reads and how long memory is kept.
  • Google Calendar data (only if you press "Connect Google Calendar"): Dory creates, moves and cancels events that it makes in your primary calendar, adds the attendees you ask for, requests a Google Meet link, and reads free/busy information to suggest times. It requests only the scopes calendar.events.owned and calendar.events.freebusy, plus your email address to confirm the account is yours.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the features you and your organization ask for: answers, summaries, reminders, meeting scheduling, receipts and reports.
  • Access follows your permissions: Dory shows information only to people who are allowed to read its source.
  • We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use Google user data or workspace content to train AI models.

3. Google user data

Dory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to schedule and manage the meetings you request. We store only an encrypted refresh token for your connection; we do not keep copies of your calendar events beyond what is needed to manage the meetings Dory created.

4. Processors and storage

Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the asia-southeast1 (Singapore) region, the service runs on Cloud Run in the same region, and secrets are kept in Google Secret Manager. Text, images and search embeddings are processed by AI models on Nebius Token Factory to generate answers. Voice calls with Dory are processed by a voice server that we host ourselves on Nebius AI Cloud. Chat platforms (Discord, Slack, LINE, Telegram, WhatsApp/Meta) process messages under their own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Team memory belongs to your organization and is kept with no expiry by default. An organization admin can set a retention period; memory older than that period is then deleted automatically.
  • Nothing is deleted when an organization stops using Dory, except at that organization's request.
  • You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the command center, or from your Google Account permissions page; this revokes access and deletes the stored token.
  • You can unlink chat accounts from the command center. To delete your data, ask your organization's admin or contact us.

6. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest, connections use HTTPS, and secrets are never written to logs.
