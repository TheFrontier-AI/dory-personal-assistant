She follows up.

Every task she hears runs on the same clock, and these are its real timings. Nobody has to remember to chase.

  1. Seconds

    First, she hears the task in chat and sends its owner a card.

  2. A day

    Then, if nobody answers the card, she nudges once after 24 hours.

  3. Three working days

    Next, a task with no deadline gets one question: when is it due?

  4. Every evening

    Last, the evening report lists what is still open.

Everything she does today

Every one of these is in the current build, and admins switch each one on or off from the command center.

  • Voice meetings

    Joins the voice room when invited, answers when called by name, and posts decisions and action items at the end.

  • Receipts and expenses

    Reads receipt photos and PDFs into a ledger, drops duplicates, and lets executives ask about spending in plain language.

  • Task follow-up and reports

    Turns action items into tasks, follows up with each owner, and sends an evening report.

  • Documents and wiki

    Remembers files the team shares, keeps a company wiki, and answers from documents each person is allowed to see.

  • Briefs and catch-up

    A personal morning and evening brief, and “what did I miss?” after a day away.

  • Approvals, polls and forms

    Expense, leave and purchase requests, votes and surveys, without leaving Discord.

  • Search

    Searches every team source at once: chat, files, the wiki, tasks and meetings.

  • Learns your company

    Teach her your glossary, SOPs and FAQ, with 14 ready-made industry packs.
