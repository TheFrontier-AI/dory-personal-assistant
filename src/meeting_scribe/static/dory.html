Then she follows up.

Every task she hears runs on the same clock, and these are its real timings. Nobody has to remember to chase.

  1. Seconds

    First, she hears the task in chat and sends its owner a card.

  2. A day

    Then, if nobody answers the card, she nudges once after 24 hours.

  3. Three working days

    Next, a task with no deadline gets one question: when is it due?

  4. Every evening

    Last, the evening report lists what is still open.

Questions, answered

Straight answers to what teams ask before they add Dory.

Which languages does Dory use?

Thai and English. The company picks one language for everything Dory says, and each person can add their own; she understands both either way. Chinese, Hindi, Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Korean and Japanese can be chosen too.

Where does Dory work?

In Discord, right away: add her to your server and mention @Dory. Organizations can also connect Slack and ClickUp, and every member can talk to her in the web command center.

What does Dory keep, and who owns it?

What your team tells Dory is kept as your company's own record, forever by default, and never mixes with another company's. An admin can choose to delete memory after a set number of days.

How do we start?

Add Dory to your Discord server and mention @Dory, or create an account and name your organization first. It takes about two minutes.

What does it cost?

Nothing during launch: every feature is free, and there is no payment in the system. Team and organization plans will be announced after launch, and trial users hear first.
